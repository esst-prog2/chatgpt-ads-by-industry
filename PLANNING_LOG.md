## HW4 Spike Definition & Context
- **Architecture Context:** Clarified MVP intent — synthetic data is an intentional architectural placeholder alongside our 1 real active account to showcase multi-industry box plots before onboarding additional real client APIs.
- **Spike Question:** Does our 1 real campaign's CTR and CPC shift more between two disjoint 7-day windows (Week 1 vs. Week 2) than the dashboard's current median metrics move between industries?
- **Success / Decision Criteria:** Compare the real campaign's 7-day swing (%) against between-industry median gaps (%). If the within-campaign swing matches or exceeds the between-industry gap, we acknowledge that single-campaign variance overlaps with industry baselines and will update the README accordingly.
- **Privacy Approval:** Owner approval granted for committing aggregated metrics only (no raw account IDs or campaign names).
